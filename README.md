# 📊 LandAlert Dashboard

The **LandAlert Dashboard** is a geospatial decision-support interface designed to visualize climate risk, land tenure records, and field reports generated by the LandAlert alerting system.

It serves governments, NGOs, planners, and land administrators by transforming satellite-driven risk analytics into actionable insights at community and parcel levels.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

The dashboard bridges the gap between:
- **Climate risk alerts** (flood, drought, heat)
- **Land tenure information** (formal and informal)
- **Governance and planning decisions**

It enables stakeholders to monitor vulnerable communities, prioritize interventions, and support land administration workflows.

---

## 🧩 Core Functions

### 1. Climate Risk Visualization
- Displays spatial layers for:
  - Flood risk
  - Drought severity
  - Urban heat stress
- Risk indices computed in Google Earth Engine are rendered as map layers
- Time-based analysis allows monitoring of seasonal and long-term trends

---

### 2. Land Parcel & STDM Integration
- Integrates **STDM (Social Tenure Domain Model)** data via QGIS
- Each land parcel stores:
  - Occupant / user name
  - Land use type
  - Coordinates
  - Contact information
- Climate risk layers are spatially linked to land parcels and communities

---

### 3. Alert & Field Report Management
- Incoming alerts from Telegram are logged and displayed
- Field reports submitted by users are:
  - Geo-referenced
  - Linked to specific parcels or settlements
- Enables validation of climate impacts on the ground

---

### 4. Community Risk Profiling
- Aggregates parcel-level data to community scale
- Identifies:
  - High-risk informal settlements
  - Climate-exposed agricultural zones
- Supports prioritization for upgrading, mitigation, and aid deployment

---

## 🗺 Data Sources

### Satellite & Climate Data
- LANDSAT
- MODIS
- CHIRPS
- ERA5
- GloFAS

### Derived Indices
- NDVI – vegetation health
- VCI – crop condition
- TCI – temperature stress
- VHI – combined vegetation stress

### Land Data
- STDM databases (QGIS)
- Community land records
- User-submitted geolocation data

---

## 🏗 System Architecture

1. **Google Earth Engine**
   - Processes satellite imagery
   - Computes climate risk indices

2. **LandAlert Backend**
   - Stores alerts, land records, and reports
   - Manages spatial relationships

3. **Dashboard Interface**
   - Interactive map viewer
   - Attribute tables and filters
   - Risk dashboards and summaries

4. **External Integrations**
   - Telegram (alerts & reports)
   - QGIS / STDM (land tenure data)

---

## 📌 Use Cases

- Government agencies monitoring climate vulnerability
- NGOs planning climate adaptation interventions
- Land administrators managing informal settlements
- Researchers analyzing climate–land interactions

## ⚠ Current Limitations

- Dependent on availability of STDM and parcel data
- Global-scale rendering may be constrained by GEE computation limits
- Requires stable internet for real-time visualization
